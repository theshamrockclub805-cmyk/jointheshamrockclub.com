Frequently asked questions

Everything businesses usually want to know before joining.

Still have questions after reviewing the packages? Use the inquiry option and our team can help you find the right fit.

Where does my membership fee go?

Membership costs help support participating high school programs. A portion of the funds generated through Shamrock Club memberships is directed toward eligible student programs, such as athletics, clubs, academic programs, arts, and other school-based initiatives.

What is the difference between the membership tiers?

Each tier offers different levels of visibility, marketing opportunities, and community engagement. Businesses can choose the package that best fits their budget and marketing goals while supporting local high school programs.

Do I have to be located near a participating high school to join?

No. Businesses of all types and locations are welcome to participate, especially those interested in connecting with local high school communities and supporting students.

How long does my membership last?

Membership terms and benefits are based on the selected tier package. Please review your membership details at the time of enrollment for specific dates and renewal information.

Can I join if I am a small or new business?

Absolutely! The Shamrock Club is designed to create opportunities for businesses of different sizes. Our tier options allow businesses to select a level of participation that works for their budget and goals.

Is the sponsorship annual?

Yes. Every single-school sponsorship runs for one year, and we will reach out ahead of renewal so you can decide whether to continue.

Can more than one business in my category sponsor the same school?

No. Category exclusivity is held at each school: one plumber, one salon, one restaurant per school, per tier. Once your category is taken at a school, it is yours for the year.

How do the corporate sponsorships work?

Corporate options provide Gold-level benefits across multiple schools — 2 schools at District Partner, 5 at District Leader, and all 10 at District Champion. They also unlock featured visibility across Oxnard USA, plus discounted ad placements, without requiring a separate Oxnard USA membership purchase.

What if I am interested but not ready to purchase online?

Use the inquiry form to request more information or a phone conversation before completing the sponsorship application.
