Membership Agreement

Version 1.1 — effective October 29, 2026

The short version: you sponsor a school through The Shamrock Club for a year at a time, you get the benefits of the tier you chose, and no competing business in your trade sponsors the same school alongside you. Membership fees are not tax-deductible charitable contributions, because you receive advertising and promotional benefits in return. Separate donations you choose to make are a different matter. You can cancel mid-year with a month's notice.

1. Who this agreement is with

This agreement is between The Shamrock Club, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, EIN 61-2052400 ("Shamrock", "we", "us"), and the business named on the form you signed ("you", "your business", "the sponsor"). It takes effect on the date you sign.

2. What your membership includes

Each tier includes everything in the tiers below it. What you receive depends on the tier recorded on your signed form:

TierIncluded
BronzeMembership card and app access, window or counter sticker, promotion on our website and in our emails, invitations to Shamrock events, and donor recognition.
SilverEverything in Bronze, plus a banner displayed at games and featured posts on our social channels.
GoldEverything in Silver, plus premium banner placement, a plaque, and an interview feature.
District tiersCoverage across multiple schools, agreed with you individually before you sign.

Physical items such as banners and plaques are produced and installed on the schedule we give you in writing. Timing depends on each school's calendar and access.

3. Category exclusivity

Your sponsorship holds one business category at each school assigned to you. For as long as your membership is active and paid, we will not sell a sponsorship in that same category at that same school to another business.

Exclusivity covers the confirmed category recorded on your membership, not every service your business may offer. It applies per school: another school may have a sponsor in your category. Exclusivity ends when your membership ends.

4. Founding partner terms

These apply only if your membership is recorded as a founding partnership:

  • Now through the end of the spring 2027 semester: no cost. Your business stays in the network at the school you are associated with.
  • The 2027–28 school year: billing begins, with advance notice before any invoice is issued.
  • The 2027–28 school year: the price recorded on your signed form is held for that school year, even if our standard pricing rises.
  • Founding Partner recognition on our website and sponsor listings for as long as your membership remains active.

Founding partner pricing of $748 per year for Silver and $1,498 per year for Gold, each a 25% reduction on standard pricing, is available only on memberships signed on or before December 15, 2026. Upgrades after that date are available at standard pricing, and your existing locked rate is unaffected.

5. Fees and payment

Standard pricing is $497 per year for Bronze, $997 for Silver and $1,997 for Gold. The amount you owe is the one recorded on your signed form.

Membership runs for a school year. We invoice in advance and will tell you before an invoice is issued. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details. We also accept cheque or cash by arrangement. Annual and monthly payment options are shown at checkout where available; a monthly plan runs for twelve payments and then stops.

If payment is more than 30 days overdue, we may pause your benefits, including your category exclusivity, after telling you first and giving you a reasonable chance to settle.

6. Tax treatment

Membership fees are not tax-deductible as charitable contributions. You receive advertising, promotional and other benefits of substantial value in return for your payment, so the payment is not a gift. We do not issue charitable contribution receipts for membership fees, and nothing we say should be read as telling you a membership fee is deductible.

Separate donations you choose to make to The Shamrock Club, over and above your membership fee and without benefits in return, are a different matter and we will acknowledge them accordingly.

Your membership fee may be deductible to your business as an ordinary advertising or marketing expense. That is a question for your own tax adviser, not for us.

7. Renewal

Memberships renew for the following school year at our standard pricing then in effect, unless you tell us otherwise. For founding partners, renewal at standard pricing begins after the 2027–28 school year, once the locked-price period has run.

We will tell you the renewal price before the new school year starts, with enough notice for you to decide.

8. Cancelling

You may cancel mid-year. We ask for at least one month's written notice — an email to staff@jointheshamrockclub.com is enough.

Your benefits continue through the notice period. Your category exclusivity ends when your membership does, and the category becomes available to other businesses. Items already produced for you, such as banners and plaques, are yours to keep.

9. If we cannot deliver something

Occasionally something we promised becomes impossible — a school leaves the program, an event is cancelled, a season is cut short. If that happens we will offer you a comparable substitute benefit or a credit against your membership, whichever you prefer. We will discuss it with you rather than decide for you.

10. Use of your name and logo

By signing, you give us permission to display your business name and logo on our website, in sponsor listings, on banners, plaques and signage, in our emails and social posts, and in materials promoting the program and the schools we support. This permission lasts while your membership is active and extends to historical records of past sponsors afterwards.

You confirm you have the right to grant this. You can ask us to stop using a particular image or to use an updated logo at any time, and we will do so for anything produced after your request.

11. What we ask of you

  • Give us accurate business information, and tell us if it changes.
  • Send us the logo and any assets we need, in reasonable time, so we can deliver what you paid for.
  • Keep your business appropriate for a high school audience. We work with schools and students, and we may decline or end a membership on those grounds. If we end one for this reason after you have paid, we will refund the unused part of your year.

12. Your information

How we handle your information is set out in our Privacy Policy. Your business name, logo, category and the school you support are published. Contact details, license or EIN numbers and payment details are not.

13. Changes to this agreement

This is version 1.1. If we change these terms, the version and date at the top of this page change with them, and the terms you agreed to are the version in effect on the date you signed. We will tell you in advance of any change that materially affects an active membership.

14. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. Any dispute will be handled in the state or federal courts located in Ventura County, California, and both of us agree to that.

Before going to court, we each agree to raise the problem directly and try in good faith to resolve it.

15. The whole agreement

This page, together with the details recorded on the form you signed, is the entire agreement between us about your membership. Typing your name on the form is your electronic signature and has the same effect as signing on paper. If any part of this agreement turns out to be unenforceable, the rest still stands.
